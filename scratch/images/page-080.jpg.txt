STANLEY CuP

Success Rate 90%

Attracts sporty chicks but not sports fan chicks

Requirements tin foil, Sharpie

Prep Time forty-five minutes

Bummers too much arts and crafts for some

me THE PLAY

1. Glue or tape a punch bowl to the top of a coffeemaker or lamp.
If it’s roughly the shape of a chess pawn or a bloated toilet
plunger, you’re good to go.

2. Cover your sculpture in tin foil. You are now in possession of
the Stanley Cup.

3. Use the Sharpie to blacken out a few teeth—just like you’d do if
there were a play called The Bumpkin (which probably wouldn’t
be very successful... unless you’d just discovered oil).

4. Carry your trophy into a bar and wait for the ladies to swoon.
