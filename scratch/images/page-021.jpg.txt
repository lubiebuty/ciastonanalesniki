HOW TO USE
THE PLAYBOOK

s you flip through The Playbook you'll notice that each
play is presented in an easy-to-follow recipe format. This
is done so that even a chump like you has a shot at glory.

Immediately following the title of the play is a profile box that
presents the following quick reference information:

Success Rate—the likelihood of “completing” the play

Attracts—the type of chick the play is designed to ensnare
Requirements—the props and/or special talents you'll need

Prep Time—how much time you need to invest in the play
Bummers—potential negatives to running the play, other than “might

totally fail”

Following the profile box are the numbered steps for each play.
Simply follow them word for word with absolutely no deviation
and you'll get laid. Maybe.
