Bouncer

Success Rate 30%, 3% in the rain

Attracts exclusive chicks

Requirements boom box, velvet ropes, clipboard

Prep Time a few hours

Bummers scoring with your target not as awesome as
watching your favorite artist live

THE PLAY

Suit up.

Find a back or side door in a location where a lot of potential
targets walk by and create a boundary around it using velvet
ropes and stanchions.

Q: Where can I get velvet ropes and stanchions?

A: They are readily available at your local movie theater or
museum. Just go and take them. If anybody asks what
you’re doing, point to your clipboard, shrug, and say,
“Maintenance.”

Choose a live album featuring your favorite (living) recording

artist and load it up in the boom box. Hit play and then “guard”
the door.
