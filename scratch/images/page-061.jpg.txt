THE PLAY

1. Travel to New York and go to the observation deck of the Em-
pire State Building.

coming.”

Why DOES THIS WORK?

For generations the observation deck of the Empire State Building
has been the place for romantic reunions with long-estranged lovers.
Provided one of the estranged lovers is running a few minutes late,
scoring here can be easy pickings.
